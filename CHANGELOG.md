## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-21

### Added

- Realtime PipeWire EQ editing in an Ink terminal UI.
- Ten editable peaking bands with precise gain, frequency, and Q controls.
- Bass shelf boost with headroom protection.
- Protected preamp and per-band bypass controls.
- Preset creation, loading, and immediate selected-preset saving.
- Safe EQ/physical-device routing with fades and volume preservation.
- Open-source project policy, CI, release automation, and contributor guidance.
