# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want PipeQ discussions to be welcoming, technically rigorous, and safe for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Participants should:

- be respectful and assume good faith;
- focus criticism on code, behavior, and evidence;
- explain disagreement clearly and constructively;
- protect other users’ privacy and credentials.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and intentional disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer through the repository’s private contact channel. Reports will be reviewed and handled proportionately, including moderation or removal from project spaces when necessary.
